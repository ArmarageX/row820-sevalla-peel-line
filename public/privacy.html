Publication notes

Privacy at The Peel Line

From The Peel Line · a practical reading series

The Peel Line is a static reading site. Its authored pages do not contain analytics, contact forms, advertising scripts or account features. The authored code does not set cookies or use browser storage such as local storage, session storage or an application database. These statements describe this site's own page behavior, not every action of the browser, hosting infrastructure or network used to reach it.

Reading does not create an account or message

There is no registration, sign-in, comment box or message submission feature. The site does not ask for a name, address, email address, password or shipment record. It has no authored intake database that stores questions or reader notes. The Contact page explains the absence of a monitored contact route; a note made in your own notebook or document is not transmitted to the publication.

Site navigation lets a reader move among articles. A menu control changes which links are displayed; it is not a tracking preference or consent setting. Because the authored site contains no analytics or advertising tracking tools, it does not provide a dashboard for opting into them, a personalized recommendation profile or a reader history maintained by the publication.

Page delivery has separate limits

Requesting a page still involves a network connection. Hosting systems, delivery infrastructure and network operators may process information such as an IP address, requested path, request time or browser-related headers while providing that connection. Their exact logging practices, retention periods and access arrangements are not established here. The publication cannot promise that no infrastructure logs exist or that all connection data disappears after a visit.

A browser may also cache downloaded pages and images or retain browsing history according to its own settings. That browser behavior is different from authored site code writing a reader profile or storing a message. The absence of site-created cookies and browser storage does not disable normal caching, bookmarks, downloads, device backups or records maintained by software outside the site.

External destinations have their own policies

Some editorial reading links lead away from The Peel Line. Following one makes a request to a separately operated website, which may use its own cookies, forms, analytics, advertising tools or account features. This page does not describe or control those systems. Review the destination's own privacy information before entering personal details or using its services.

Depending on browser settings and the link's behavior, a destination may receive information about where a visit came from. Do not assume that leaving a static article makes the next site equally limited in its data practices. An external link offers further reading, not a shared privacy arrangement or a transfer into a contact service operated here.

Keep shipping information out of unnecessary copies

Packing work can involve names, addresses, barcodes and shipment identifiers. Those details are not required to read these articles, and this site supplies no place to submit them. When keeping private equipment notes or seeking help elsewhere, limit copied shipment information to what the legitimate task requires. The general examples and AI-generated editorial illustrations do not invite readers to upload real labels or customer records.

For the publication's scope and fictional narrator, see About. Return to the main article to continue reading. This privacy description is limited to the authored static features described above; it is not a guarantee about third-party systems or a claim to offer data-access and deletion services for records the publication does not hold.
